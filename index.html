Empowering Nonprofits with Tech Solution

Decades of Experience, a Passion for Purpose

Bee Lite is an initiative by Fat Beehive, the UK’s leading digital agency for charities and non-profits. With over 26 years of experience building impactful charity websites, we understand the unique needs of the sector.

Since 97% of UK charities are considered ‘small’, we know that many are doing amazing work with limited resources—and that a fully bespoke website isn’t always within reach.

That’s why we created Bee Lite: a more affordable solution designed specifically for small charities, offering two flexible options to suit your budget without compromising on quality.
